### 🚗 VAT Margin Scheme Spreadsheet (Used Car Sales)

This spreadsheet is designed for businesses dealing with used car sales under the VAT Margin Scheme in the UK.

It provides a structured way to track:
- Income from car sales and additional services  
- Expenses (including cost of vehicles and operational costs)  
- VAT calculations specific to the margin scheme  

## 💡 Purpose

The file simplifies VAT margin calculations by separating standard VAT transactions from margin-based adjustments, reducing the risk of errors and improving reporting clarity.

## 🛠️ Skills Demonstrated

- Financial data structuring  
- VAT logic implementation (including margin scheme adjustments)  
- Spreadsheet design for real business use  
- Process simplification for accounting workflows
